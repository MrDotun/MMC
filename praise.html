Category Excellent (3 pts) Good (2 pts) Needs Practice (1 pt) Score

Instructions: Pair students up to play a 15-minute game. Grade them using the rubric below.

Category Excellent (3 pts) Good (2 pts) Needs Practice (1 pt) Score
Development Brings out minor pieces and controls center. Develops some pieces, but moves too many pawns. Only moves pawns or one piece repeatedly.
Trades & Notation Makes smart trades; attempts to record moves. Makes equal trades; struggles with notation. Gives away pieces for free.
Sportsmanship Shakes hands, quiet focus, says "good game". Mostly focused, respectful. Distracted, loud, or upset.
