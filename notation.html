Record the moves shown on the board.

How to Record Moves

A piece will move from GREEN to RED.

Example 1: A Knight moves to f3 → Type: Nf3

Example 2: A Bishop captures on d5 → Type: Bxd5

Example 3: A Rook moves to e8 and gives Checkmate → Type: Re8#

Always use capital letters for pieces (K, Q, R, B, N).
