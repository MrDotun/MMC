Piece Guide

How the pieces move

Watch each short animation. Green squares = where it can go. Perfect for kids & new players.

♟ Pawn

Moves forward one square (or two on its first move). Captures diagonally.

♜ Rook

Moves any number of squares horizontally or vertically — like a plus sign +.

♞ Knight

Moves in an L shape (2 then 1). The only piece that jumps over others!

♝ Bishop

Moves any number of squares diagonally. Stays on the same color squares.

♛ Queen

The strongest piece! Moves like a rook and bishop combined — any direction.

♚ King

Moves only one square in any direction. Protect your king — that’s the goal of the game!

Special moves

Castling · En passant · Promotion

Rules kids meet after the basic pieces

Castling

King moves two squares toward a rook; the rook jumps to the other side. Only if neither has moved and the path is clear.

En passant

If a pawn jumps two squares and lands beside your pawn, you may capture it as if it moved only one — only on the next move.

Promotion

When a pawn reaches the last rank, it becomes a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight. Almost always a Queen!
